WIN-066 — NH Heat Excess: +0.34 W/m² Asymmetry
Category: Climate / Energy Balance
Date claimed: 2026-04-04
Status on dome site: CONFIRMED

What we predicted:
The Ovoid Tilt geometry of the Closed Toroidal Ovoid predicts a persistent Northern
Hemisphere energy absorption excess due to the narrower (higher-firmament) northern cavity
receiving more concentrated solar circuit energy per unit area.

What the data returned:
CERES 2025 satellite radiometry data confirms the Northern Hemisphere absorbs +0.34 W/m²
more energy than the Southern Hemisphere on an annual basis.

Dome vs Globe:
Globe requires approximate solar symmetry between hemispheres (a small asymmetry from
land/ocean distribution is expected, but the observed magnitude and persistence exceed the
land-albedo explanation). The ovoid tilt provides a clean geometric mechanism.

Notes (our review):
- The dome explicitly acknowledges the land/ocean asymmetry explanation but claims the
  observed +0.34 W/m²/decade exceeds it. This is the claim our review challenges (see
  detail_evidence for WIN-066 in wins.json): standard physics (Loeb et al. 2025, PNAS)
  attributes the trend to aerosols, ice loss, and differential land-mass distribution
  (~39% NH vs ~19% SH), with no dome geometry required.
- The 39%/19% land-mass distribution figures are derived from spherical-Earth measurement
  (total land area per hemisphere from global GIS datasets). Under any area-preserving
  projection, these percentages are invariant — the ratio is a property of actual land
  area, not of projection geometry. The dome's implicit alternative (higher-firmament
  geometry) would require specifying a different area definition, which it does not do.
